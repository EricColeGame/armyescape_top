## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes an Army Escape Discord different from regular gaming servers?
An **Army Escape Discord** is typically founded and managed by military veterans and official game Sherpas. These servers focus on structured communication, tactical teamwork, zero-tolerance policies for toxicity, and regular support initiatives for veteran mental health charities like Mission 22.

### Do I need prior military service to join an Army Escape Discord?
No. While these communities are founded on military teamwork values and support veterans, the vast majority are open to all civilian gamers who respect community rules, value disciplined communication, and want to learn extraction mechanics.

### How do I request an official Sherpa session in the server?
Most servers feature a dedicated Sherpa or coaching channel. You can open a support ticket detailing your in-game level, the specific maps or quests you need assistance with, and your active time zone. A BSG-appointed Sherpa or veteran mentor will coordinate a raid schedule with you.

### Are there skill requirements to join tournaments in these communities?
Most monthly community events offer tiered divisions ranging from beginner-friendly extraction challenges to high-tier PvP showdowns. Check the server's event announcements channel for specific entry rules, squad sizes, and prize details.
