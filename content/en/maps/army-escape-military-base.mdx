## Breaching the Exterior Defenses

The exterior perimeter is secured by well-armed mutant sentries. You have three primary ways to gain entry through the blast doors.

1. **The Radio Distraction Gambit**: Using your radio on the perimeter lets you broadcast a fake distress call. This tricks three of the four guards (including the rocket launcher specialist) into abandoning their posts, awarding you 1,500 bonus XP.
2. **Brute Force Assault**: Engage all four guards directly. If you secured support from the Brotherhood elders, three crack assault paladins will assist you in wiping out the perimeter guards.
3. **Covert Infiltration**: Wearing Children of the Cathedral (COC) robes allows you to walk past the exterior sentries without triggering an alarm.

## Navigating Facility Floors: Levels 1 Through 3

Once inside, understanding the inner layout prevents deadly dead-ends and saves vital minutes when orchestrating your eventual evacuation. For detailed game lore and system mechanics, consult the official [Fallout Wiki on Fandom](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Destroy_the_source_of_the_Mutants).

### Level 1: Security and Robot Control

Level 1 contains security consoles that dictate the automated defenses of the entire compound:
* **Emitter Override**: Use the southeastern console to link emitter frequencies to your radio, disabling fields remotely.
* **Robotics Reprogramming**: Access the terminal near the elevator to switch automated sentries to friendly status. Alter pest control parameters to "Large Pests" to eliminate patrolling guards on Level 2 automatically.

### Force Field Mechanics

Dealing with security emitters is critical to prevent getting trapped.

| Field Type | Visual Color | Disabling Method | Hazard Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Temporal Field** | Yellow | Repair skill on emitter (do not use basic tools) or remote radio hack | Blocks path; can trap players if keypads reset |
| **Lethal Field** | Red | High-explosive detonation in adjacent hex | Deals severe direct health damage when crossed |

### Level 2 & Level 3: Armory and Trap Corridors

* **Level 2 (Supply Depot)**: Rich in ammunition, medical supplies, and energy cells. Avoid getting trapped between yellow fields without explosive charges.
* **Level 3 (Security Barracks)**: Features a large chamber filled with hidden landmines and tripwires. Characters with high Traps skill can easily mark and disarm these hazards. Defeat the elite defenders Flip and Krupper before descending to Level 4.

## Executing the Army Escape Military Base Sequence

The culmination of the mission requires setting the destruction countdown and sprinting back to the surface. An error in judgment here will trap your character inside the blast radius.

### Terminal Configuration Options

When accessing the primary console in the vat overlook room, your Science and Intelligence attributes determine how easily you can set the timer:

| Player Attribute Profile | Terminal Option Selected | Countdown Duration | Yellow Field Status | Risk Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Science (>75%)** | Second Option from Bottom | 300 Seconds (5 Min) | Automatically Lowered | Lowest Risk |
| **Tech Keycard Used** | Decrypted Override | 300 Seconds (5 Min) | Automatically Lowered | Lowest Risk |
| **Standard Override** | Top Command Lines | 60–180 Seconds | Remain Active | High Risk |
| **Low Intelligence (&lt;4)** | Manual Explosive Rigging | Detonator Timer | Manual Clearance Needed | Extreme Risk |

### Step-by-Step Evacuation Checklist

1. **Activate Countdown**: Choose the longest duration (300 seconds) to ensure plenty of buffer time for elevator transitions.
2. **Ascend to Level 3**: Avoid remaining combat; run directly past fallen obstacles and stick to previously cleared mine corridors.
3. **Traverse Level 2 to Level 1**: If emitters reactivated, use your pre-tuned radio to disable fields instantly.
4. **Surface Exit**: Cross the ground floor threshold to reach the world map before the timer hits zero.

Completing this thrilling run grants an massive 10,000 XP reward and brings you one step closer to securing the wasteland's future.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What causes the Army Escape military base timer bug?
In certain unpatched versions of the classic engine, selecting specific terminal prompts in rapid succession can cause the countdown timer to drop instantly to zero. To avoid this game-breaking issue, always save your game immediately before accessing the vats console and select the second prompt from the bottom.

### Can I complete the Army Escape military base without fighting the Lieutenant?
Yes. The Lieutenant's quarters are located on the right wing of Level 4, while the vats control room is accessible by moving past Vat Team 9's wing. By using high Sneak skills, a Stealth Boy, or COC robes, you can trigger the terminal countdown and execute your escape without ever initiating dialogue or combat with the Lieutenant.

### What should I do if I get trapped behind a yellow force field during an Army Escape military base run?
If you are locked behind a yellow emitter on Level 2 without an active radio link, drop a primed timed explosive or dynamite directly in the adjacent hex to permanently destroy the emitter. Always carry at least two explosive charges to avoid being soft-locked.
