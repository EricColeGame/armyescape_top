## Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Failing an infiltration run on the **Army Escape military school** challenge usually traces back to a few critical operational errors. Review these frequent pitfalls to improve your overall survival rate:

*   **Ignoring Camera Sweep Angles:** Security cameras operate on fixed horizontal intervals. Rushing beneath them while the lens is resetting triggers immediate lockdowns.
*   **Neglecting Guard Patrol Resets:** When an alarm clears, guards do not immediately return to their baseline loops. Wait an extra 10 seconds to confirm their standard pathing has resumed.
*   **Over-relying on High-Profile Transport:** While driving an academy vehicle through the front gate looks tempting, watchtower snipers deal lethal engine damage before you clear the final barricade.
*   **Dropping Quest Items:** Dropping key items like the wire cutters or valve wheel to pick up non-essential weapons will stall your run at the final perimeter fence.

By memorizing patrol rotations, prioritizing stealth tools over raw firepower, and executing clean zone transitions, mastering the **Army Escape military school** scenario becomes a methodical, highly rewarding tactical achievement.

### What is the safest extraction route in the Army Escape military school map?

### Where can I find the high-voltage generator to disable the perimeter fence?
The auxiliary generator is housed inside the small concrete utility shed located directly behind the motor pool maintenance bay. You will need either a lockpick or the maintenance keycard found in the administration breakroom to access the control panel.

### How do guard alert states work in the Army Escape military school challenge?
Guards operate across three distinct awareness phases: Normal Patrol (White), Investigating/Suspicious (Yellow), and Combat Alert (Red). If a guard enters Yellow status, throwing an object in the opposite direction will draw them away from your path. Red status initiates a sector-wide lockdown, dispatching additional sentries to high-traffic exits.
