## Phase 1: The Detention Barracks

Your mission begins inside a high-security holding cell. The objective in Phase 1 is to acquire basic lockpicking tools, bypass the cell door, and neutralize the corridor camera without raising an alarm.

```
[Cell B-4] ---> [Locker Corridor] ---> [Guard Substation] ---> [Courtyard Gate]
```

### Step-by-Step Cell Breakdown

1. **Inspect the Bunk Bed**: Lift the worn mattress on the lower bunk to reveal a **Bent Wire Frame**.
2. **Examine the Sink**: Tap the drainage pipe under the basin. Use the loose bolt to loosen the pipe clamp, obtaining the **Metal Washer**.
3. **Combine Items**: Open your inventory, combine the **Bent Wire Frame** with the **Metal Washer** to fashion a **Makeshift Lockpick**.
4. **Pick the Cell Lock**: Interact with the keyhole on the barred door. Rotate the pick until tension clicks at the 2 o'clock and 8 o'clock positions.

## Phase 4: Perimeter Breach and Final Extraction

The final chapter of this **Army Escape walkthrough** covers the sprint to the extraction zone. The base is on high alert once you trigger the main perimeter gate controls, making speed and execution critical.

### The Final Escape Route Options

Community reports indicate there are two primary extraction paths available depending on the items you secured during earlier phases:

| Route Option | Required Inventory Items | Difficulty | Advantages / Trade-offs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Route A: Military Truck Breach** | Truck Battery, Diesel Canister, Ignition Key | Medium | Fast getaway, bypasses sniper towers, but triggers noisy engine alarm. |
| **Route B: Drainage Canal Sneak** | Crowbar, Flashlight, Heavy Bolt Cutters | High | Silent escape, avoids all vehicular alerts, but features complex water valve puzzles. |

### Completing the Final Gate Puzzle

If executing Route A:
1. Insert the fully charged **Truck Battery** into the engine bay.
2. Fasten the cables using your **12mm Wrench**.
3. Pour the **Diesel Canister** into the fuel tank.
4. Insert the **Ignition Key** recovered from the Officer Safe.
5. Ram the exterior wooden barricade to complete the mission successfully.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where do I find the armory key in Army Escape?
The armory key is located inside the Officer Safe within the Command Center. To unlock the safe, solve the shift cipher using the four-digit date code (`7193`) derived from the Commander’s operational shift board.

### What should I do if the courtyard searchlight spots me?
If you are caught by a searchlight, immediately deploy a smoke grenade or dive behind the nearest cargo container. Break line of sight for 10 seconds until the yellow alert status returns to normal before resuming your path outlined in this **Army Escape walkthrough**.

### How do I bypass the laser grid in the Radio Room corridor?
Access the circuit breaker panel on the adjacent wall, pull the main power lever down, and reconnect the colored wires to their matching numbered terminals (Red to 3, Blue to 1, Yellow to 4) to safely disable the infrared beams.

### Can you finish Army Escape without triggering any alarms?
Yes. By following Route B through the drainage canal and using the screwdriver and bolt cutters instead of the military truck, you can achieve a zero-alarm ghost extraction rating.
